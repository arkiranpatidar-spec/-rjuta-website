Before appointing a broker

Six questions that reveal
more than a sales pitch.

  1. 01How many transactions have you closed in my target society during the last 12 months?
  2. 02Can you show evidence of your last three comparable transactions?
  3. 03What were their asking prices versus actual closing prices?
  4. 04Are you receiving brokerage from the buyer, seller, developer—or more than one party?
  5. 05Who are you representing in this transaction?
  6. 06What is your exact commission and when does it become payable?

Method & limitations

Evidence before inference.

Status logic
Revoked/cancelled if matched to the official revocation snapshot; otherwise expired if the displayed expiry date has passed; otherwise active where a usable expiry is present. Duplicate or incomplete rows are marked uncertain.

Data minimization
No phone numbers, personal email addresses or private information are published. V1 contains only regulatory identity fields needed for verification.

Known source limitation
The official Gurugram revoked-agent endpoint returned no records on 10 September 2026. Absence from that snapshot is not proof that no adverse order exists.

Publication permission
Haryana RERA’s terms say portal material may be reproduced after permission. The full-data publication and automatic daily refresh should remain gated until written permission is obtained.
